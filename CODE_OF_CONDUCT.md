# Code of conduct

Parley is a small project, and we'd like it to stay a good place to ask questions and propose changes.

- Be kind and assume good intent. Disagree about ideas, not people.
- No harassment, insults or personal attacks, in issues, pull requests or anywhere else the project lives.
- Keep other people's private information private.

If something here goes wrong, tell the maintainer privately through [Scott Meyer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Scott-Meyer) or a private security report, and it will be dealt with. Contributions or comments that don't follow this may be edited or removed, and repeated problems may lead to being blocked from the project.

This follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/).
